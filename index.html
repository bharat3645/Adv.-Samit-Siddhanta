About the Counsel

Samit Siddhanta — Advocate-on-Record before the Supreme Court of India

Enrolled with the Bar Council of Delhi in 1994 and designated an Advocate-on-Record, Samit Siddhanta has built a practice defined by rigour, integrity and an unwavering commitment to his clients' cause. He appears regularly before the Supreme Court, the Delhi High Court and tribunals across the country.

His chambers combine the discipline of seasoned litigation with a modern, research-driven approach — pairing sharp legal strategy with clear, candid counsel. Whether the matter is a constitutional question of national consequence or a deeply personal family dispute, every client receives the same measured attention.

  • LL.M. — Constitutional & Administrative Law

Professional Journey

Three decades in the service of justice

1994

Enrolled at the Bar

Began practice with the Bar Council of Delhi, apprenticing under leading senior counsel in civil and criminal chambers.

2001

Designated Advocate-on-Record

Cleared the rigorous AOR examination, earning the right to file and argue matters directly before the Supreme Court.

2009

Founded Siddhanta Chambers

Established an independent practice in New Delhi, building a team focused on constitutional and commercial litigation.

Landmark Constitutional Matters
